# PMID_40666313

## Coverage notes

The study used targeted sequencing (MIP) to assess multiple antimalarial resistance genes, including:

- k13 (broad coverage across the gene, with emphasis on the propeller domain)
- pfcrt
- pfmdr1
- pfdhfr
- pfdhps

Mutation data are reported as aggregated frequencies in supplementary tables, with a corresponding genotype dataset referenced but not directly available. A linked GitHub repository appears to host the underlying data. The k13 data reflect a set of predefined loci rather than continuous coverage across the gene.

---

## Extracted markers

The following positions have been extracted and encoded in counts.csv:

- pfcrt: 76
- pfmdr1: 86
- k13 (WHO candidate and validated set):

441, 446, 449, 458, 469, 476, 481, 493, 515, 527, 537, 538, 539, 543, 553, 561, 568, 574, 580, 622, 675


Data on crt, mdr1 and k13 580 and 622I were provided directly by the study authors. For other k13 WHO positions, WT was imputed based on the smallest coverage over the two reported k13 positions. This assumes that sequencing coverage included all WHO positions, and that the absence of recorded mutations in these other positions implies that there were no such mutations.
